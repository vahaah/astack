## How each step runs

- Step 1 starts with the PR #17 edit. A fixture gains three plain lines in D4 and three in its Category composition, and only the D4 lines fail.
- Other fixtures edit or add a fenced line of D1, as in ADR 0025:29-31, or add `#### Verification` with a line under D2. Each exits 1.
- The property generator edits plain lines, fenced lines and base spans, and adds proof headings. Only dated blocks and base span edits pass.
- The rule uses `adrProse` only to find headings. A proof span is a `####` heading with a proof title, up to the next heading of level 1 to 4.
- A base span is a proof span or the Domain and effects or Invariant audit part, and it drops. A head span drops only when the base holds a span with the same heading under the same decision.
- Then the rule diffs the remaining raw lines. A removed line fails, except the Status and `Amended by:` lines.
- An added line passes when it is blank, an `Amended by:` line, a `Superseded by NNNN` or `Deprecated` Status, or in a dated block.
- A dated block starts with `> Note YYYY-MM-DD:` or `> Amended YYYY-MM-DD:`, and each next line starts with `>`. The date is a real day on or after the `Date:` of the ADR.
- The rules match ADR files by number, so a new slug keeps its history. A deleted ADR fails unless the base holds it as Proposed.
- The relation rule reads the `Supersedes:`, `Amends:` and `Amended by:` lines. A number starts a group, and each `D<n>` after it belongs to that number.
- An `Amended by:` entry passes when the named ADR has an Amends line, a text `ADR NNNN` or a link to the target. A superseded ADR needs no entry.
- The index rule reads each row as a linked number, a title and a status. The title is the H1 after `NNNN. `.
- The template needs Status Proposed or Accepted, `Date:`, `Supersedes:` and `Amends:` lines, and an optional `Ticket:`.
- Its H2 parts are Context, Decisions with a `### D1.`, Domain and effects, Consequences and Alternatives considered, in that order.
- `Terms.` names glossary terms, or `none`.
